01 · Who we are

Every success story has a list of failures.

Usually those stories stay hidden. We bring them into the open.

The Failure Club is a student-led organization at Virginia Tech that changes how students think about failure.

You've seen the stories: founders who went bankrupt before they made it, engineers whose first prototypes fell apart, people who got rejected from dozens of internships before landing the one that mattered. The failures are always part of the story. They just rarely get talked about.

We're a community of students from every major who share setbacks openly, analyze what went wrong, and help each other try again, so the next generation of Hokies doesn't repeat mistakes someone else already made.

Failure isn't the end of the story. It's the useful part.

02 · Our mission
Through storytelling, workshops, project showcases, and professional speakers, we create a community where setbacks are analyzed, lessons are shared, and students are encouraged to take meaningful risks.

05 · What you get

Real career value.

Membership pays off on your résumé, in interviews, and in who you meet.

Résumé reviews

Feedback from upperclassmen and alumni who've been through recruiting.

Interview prep

Mock interviews, including the question everyone gets: "Tell me about a time you failed."

Real connections

Meet the recruiters, alumni, and professors who speak at our events.

Leadership roles

A new club means real positions open to members, even as freshmen.

Analysis skills

Speaking experience

The interview question

"Tell me about a time you failed."

Almost every interviewer asks it, and most students don't have a good answer. Our members will.
